      How this works. An automated check runs every hour. It reads official sources (Israel Home Front Command, IDF, US Embassy Jerusalem, CENTCOM, White House), major news outlets and prediction markets, and updates this page. Iranian state media and social-media posts count only as claims until a second independent source confirms them. Alerts go out by Teams and email when the main estimate reaches 7% or a hard trigger occurs (a strike order, an embassy departure order, a Home Front Command change, an airspace closure, US casualties or a confirmed large-scale strike).
      Why 7%. It is the lower of the estimates made on the eves of the last two attacks (12 Jun 2025: 14%; 27 Feb 2026: 7%).
