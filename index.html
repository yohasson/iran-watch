      🎯 How good is the strike forecast itself?

      6replays of past dates, run partly blind (2 dates shortly before real attacks, 4 calm dates)
      5% · 3%7-day figure on 6 Jun 2025 (a week before the June 2025 attack) and 15 Apr 2024 (4 days before the April 2024 Israeli strike)
      1.5–10%same 7-day figure on the 4 calm dates

      Honest answer: the percentage alone could not tell an attack eve from a calm day. Read today's figure as "background risk", not as a countdown.

      What did mark the real attacks, 1–4 days ahead: US staff and family drawdowns, a fixed diplomatic trigger (an IAEA censure or a deadline) and a direct leader-to-leader call. Loud threats, cabinet votes and market prices did not. That is why the warning signs matter more than small moves in the number.

      🧮 How the main estimate is built

      The main figure blends two independent views with equal weight: what the news and official sources say (news signals, E) and what betting markets imply (markets, M). When the two disagree, the truth is probably in between – today they span the range shown above. E moves only when the check finds a new signal; M follows Polymarket prices.

      📖 How this works

      An automated check runs about every hour. It reads official sources (Israel Home Front Command, IDF, US Embassy Jerusalem, CENTCOM, White House), major news outlets and prediction markets. Iranian state media and social-media posts count only as claims until a second independent source confirms them. Alerts go out by Teams and email when the main estimate reaches 7% or a hard trigger occurs (strike order, embassy departure order, Home Front Command change, airspace closure, US casualties or a confirmed large-scale strike).

      Why 7%. It is the lower of the estimates made on the eves of the last two attacks (12 Jun 2025: 14%; 27 Feb 2026: 7%). Your personal alert line in Settings only changes this page, not the Teams/email alerts.

      Update now starts a full check of news, official sources, markets and your flight. The first time on each device it asks for your password; after that it does not ask again on that device. The page updates by itself when the check finishes (about 3 minutes). The full check also runs every hour on its own.
